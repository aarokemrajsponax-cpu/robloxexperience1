# Product icons (Build 87)

512 x 512 PNGs, one for every product and pass in `src/shared/ProductConfig.luau`, made by
`python3 tools/art/product_icons.py`. Everything that matters sits inside the circle, so a round
crop loses nothing.

**None of these has been uploaded or assigned.** To use one: Creator Hub -> your experience ->
Monetization -> Developer Products (or Passes) -> the product with the name below -> its icon ->
upload the file. A pass and the product that stands in for it share one picture.
